Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Remote Mouse Keyboard Joystick ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your information when you use it to control a computer or laptop from your Android device.

2. How the App Works

The app turns your phone into a mouse, keyboard, and joystick/gamepad for your computer, together with a companion program installed on the computer. They connect over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or USB cable. Your privacy is our priority:

  • Direct connection: Your input (cursor movements, key presses, button and joystick actions) is sent directly from your phone to your own computer.
  • No servers: We do not operate servers that receive, relay, or store your input or connection data.
  • Computer companion: The app works with a small companion program that runs on your computer. It sets up the connection with your phone and receives your input so your computer can act on it. This communication goes directly between your phone and your computer over the connection you choose (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or USB cable).
  • No data sent to us: The companion program does not send your input, files, or computer activity to us or to any third party. It does not read your files or record your screen, and it only acts on the commands you send from the app.
  • No account required: You can use the app without creating an account or signing in.

3. Permissions We Request

The app may ask for the following Android permissions. They are used only for the features described:

  • Bluetooth and Nearby devices: to find and connect to your computer over Bluetooth.
  • Wi-Fi / local network access: to find and connect to your computer on your local network.
  • USB access: to communicate with your computer over a cable.
  • Internet access: used to display advertisements.

4. Information We Collect

  • Support contact: If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any details you provide. This is used only to assist you and is deleted once your request is resolved.

5. Third-Party Services (AdMob)

This app is free and shows advertisements using Google AdMob. To serve relevant ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and use certain data, such as your device advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and app information. AdMob may use this data to show personalized ads, depending on your settings and region.

AdMob is an independent third-party provider. Its data collection and use are governed by its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Your Control

App settings and paired device information remain on your device. You can erase them by clearing the app's storage in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the app. You can also revoke any permission at any time in your Android settings, although some features may then stop working.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
